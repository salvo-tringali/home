1st International Workshop
on Power Semigroups
September 21, 2025 • School of Mathematical Sciences, Hebei Normal University

The workshop is focused on recent developments in the theory of power semigroups and related topics. The workshop with the details of the event and the abstracts of the talks can be downloaded from booklet from herehere.

Plenary talks

Felix Gotti (MIT, USA)
Maximal Common Divisors in Power Monoids (slides)

Laura Cossu (University of Cagliari, Italy)
Power Monoids in a New Framework for Factorization (slides)

Pedro A. García-Sánchez (University of Granada, Spain) – Online
Isomorphism problems for ideals of numerical semigroups (slides)

Balint Rago (University of Graz, Austria) – Online
On power monoids and their automorphisms (slides)

Andreas Reinhart (University of Graz, Austria) – Online
