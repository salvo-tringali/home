The Open Seminar

The Open Seminar provides a platform for students and researchers to present their work, discuss new ideas, and explore a broad range of mathematical topics in a collaborative and friendly environment.

The seminar is hosted by the School of Mathematical Sciences at Hebei Normal University (HebNU) and organized by Salvatore Tringali (contact details) and Liping Yuan. It features a mix of guests and members of the local academic community as speakers. Unless differently specified, all talks are

Thursday 4:30–5:30 PM (Beijing time) · HebNU School of Mathematical Sciences, Room D203 or D204

To enable participants from around the world, we stream the seminar on Zoom via the link:

https://us06web.zoom.us/j/86763384947?pwd=qXhOzOcHvaaiw2jqADI8iqNavdgm14.1

To receive announcements about upcoming talks, please send an email to one of the organizers. Prospective speakers are strongly encouraged to submit both the TeX file and a PDF version of their abstract, including a short academic bio, using the template available here.
