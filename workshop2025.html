September 21, 2025 • School of Mathematical Sciences, Hebei Normal University

The workshop focuses on recent developments in the theory of power semigroups and related topics. It is the first international event of its kind to date. Further information, including the abstracts of the talks, can be found in the booklet. The organizers acknowledge support from the Natural Science Foundation of Hebei Province through grant A2023205045.
