Literature on Power Semigroups

This page collects references related to power semigroups, from the earliest works to recent developments. It was originally created by Salvatore Tringali (the current administrator) with the assistance of Zhengliang Yuan in the spring–summer of 2026, when Z.Y. was a first-year graduate student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Hebei Normal University.

The bibliography is organized chronologically by decade. The list is intended as a working resource and may be incomplete. Corrections and suggestions are very welcome (contact us).
