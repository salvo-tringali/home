Literature on Power Semigroups

This page collects references related to power semigroups, from the earliest works to recent developments. It was created by Salvatore Tringali with the assistance of Zhengliang Yuan in the spring–summer of 2026, when Z.Y. was a first-year graduate student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Hebei Normal University.

The bibliography is organized chronologically by decade. The list is intended as a working resource and may be incomplete. A green checkmark (✔) indicates that a copy of the paper is available to me, while a red cross (✘) means that I do not currently have access to it. A test tube (🧪) marks items that are available to me only as preprints (typically on arXiv). Corrections, suggestions for additional items not currently included in the list, and help in obtaining copies of items marked with a red cross are very welcome (contact me).
