Salvatore Tringali
School of Mathematical Sciences • Hebei Normal University (HebNU)

I am a Full Professor of Mathematics at the School of Mathematical Sciences of Hebei Normal University (HebNU), where I have been since February 2019.

I consider myself an algebraist. A recurring theme in my work is the interplay between arithmetic combinatorics and the structural properties of algebraic objects. I am particularly interested in the study of factorization (in semigroups, rings, etc.) and of setwise extensions of algebraic objects (power semigroups, power monoids, etc.). I also have a sustained interest in number theory, discrete mathematics, and functional equations.

Before joining HebNU, I held research and teaching positions at the University of Graz (as a Lise Meitner Fellow and lecturer), Nankai University (as a visiting professor), École Polytechnique (as a postdoc), Texas A&M University at Qatar (as a postdoc), and Sorbonne University · Campus Paris VI (as a Marie Curie Fellow).

In September 2025, my students and I organized the first international workshop on power semigroups. Further details about the event can be found here.
