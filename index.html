Salvatore Tringali
School of Mathematical Sciences • Hebei Normal University (HebNU)

I am a Full Professor of Mathematics at the School of Mathematical Sciences of Hebei Normal University (HebNU), where I have been since February 2019. I regularly teach courses in Number Theory, Graph Theory, Combinatorics, and Scientific Writing to undergraduate, master’s, and PhD students in Mathematics and Computer Science.

Research

My research lies in algebra, with a focus on power algebras and factorization theory (broadly understood) in semigroups, rings, and algebras. A recurring theme of my work is the interplay between arithmetic combinatorics and the structural properties of algebraic objects.

I also have a sustained interest in discrete mathematics, functional equations, and group and semigroup theory.

Background

Before joining Hebei Normal University, I held research and teaching positions at the University of Graz (as a Lise Meitner Fellow, visiting researcher, and lecturer), Nankai University (visiting professor), École Polytechnique (postdoctoral researcher), and Texas A&M University at Qatar (postdoctoral researcher).

I obtained a PhD in Mathematics from Université de Lyon under the supervision of Alain Plagne and François Hennecart, after an earlier PhD in computational electromagnetics from Università Mediterranea. I was also awarded a Marie Curie Intra-European Fellowship, which marked my transition from applied electromagnetics to pure mathematics.
