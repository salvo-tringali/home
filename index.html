Salvatore Tringali's homepage

Friends call me Salvo. I am a full professor of mathematics at the School of Mathematical Sciences of Hebei Normal University (HebNU), where I have been since February 2019.

A recurring theme in my research is the interplay between arithmetic combinatorics and the structural properties of algebraic objects, such as semigroups and rings. I am particularly interested in the study of power semigroups and factorizations (broadly understood).

Before joining HebNU, I was a visiting professor at the Center for Combinatorics, Nankai University (Tianjin, China); a Lise Meitner fellow and lecturer at the Dept. of Mathematics and Scientific Computing, University of Graz (Austria); a postdoc at the Centre de Mathématiques Laurent Schwartz, École Polytechnique (Palaiseau, France), and at Texas A&M University at Qatar (Doha, Qatar); and a Marie Curie Fellow at the Laboratoire Jacques-Louis Lions, Sorbonne University (Paris, France).

In September 2025, I coorganized the first international workshop on power semigroups. Further details about the event can be found here.
